# Draft — Life Quest (Schedule + Pet)

**Working names:** Life Quest · Planimal · Dodo List

**One-liner (10 words):** "Turn real-life tasks into quests that keep your pet alive."

**Problem / utility:** To-do apps are boring, so nobody opens them. Life Quest wraps a quest/dungeon RPG around your real schedule: tasks become quests, your week is the dungeon, and finishing things keeps your pet companion alive and leveling up.

**Users:** Students who procrastinate or love RPGs. Multi-user logins let friends compare dungeon progress — most rival apps are offline/single-user, so that's a gap.

**Similar products:** Taskoria, QuestTodo, Habit Slayer, Dungeon-Do, Levla, Habitica.
**Why different:** Most of these make *you* the hero. Here the pet/companion is the thing at stake — you can't feed or level it without finishing real quests. Plus real accounts and friend comparison.

**Draft features:**
- Schedule tasks → they become quests with difficulty tiers
- Your week = a dungeon: clear floors, defeat a weekly boss
- Completing quests → XP + loot → feed and upgrade your pet companion
- Missed quests = pet takes damage (decide: dies vs. just sad)
- Logins, per-user dungeons, friend leaderboard

**What you learn:** Full-stack CS + game-design thinking (progression, rewards, balance).

**Feasibility / risks:**
- High — laptop-only, no hardware
- Risk: crowded market → the pet-at-stake + accounts angle must be visible in the demo
- Risk: scope creep (animations, shop, gear) → cap early
- Scope note: quest *framing* is the plan, not real-time combat (that's a much bigger build)
